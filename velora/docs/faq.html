FAQ

How do I get Velora?

Download Velora Server for your Mac. The apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro are in TestFlight beta.

Does Velora need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything runs on your Mac and your network. Metadata and AI providers are optional. See Privacy.

Will Velora change or move my files?

No. Velora only reads your media folders.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

Velora is made for Apple silicon. On-device features such as Create Audio need it.

Why Apple devices only?

So every app can be truly native. Other devices can use the web app. See Why Velora.

Is Velora open source?

No. Velora is closed source, free to download during the beta.

Can I use audio made with Create Audio commercially?

Not with Phonon 40M, which is CC BY-NC (non-commercial).
