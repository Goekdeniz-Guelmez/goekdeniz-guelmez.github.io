Privacy

Velora is self-hosted. Your media, your accounts and your watch history live on your Mac. There's no Velora cloud and no telemetry.

What can leave your network, and when

FeatureSent toWhat is sentWhen
Movie and TV metadataTMDBTitle, year, season and episodeOnly if you add a TMDB key
Music metadata and coversMusicBrainz, Cover Art ArchiveArtist, album, trackOnly if enabled
Book metadata and coversOpen LibraryTitle, author, ISBNOnly if enabled
Create Audio script stepThe AI provider your admin choseText of the sources being turned into audioOnly with a cloud provider. Nativ and Ollama stay on your Mac.
Phonon voice downloadHugging FaceDownload requestWhen voices are downloaded
MCPThe assistant you connectResults of the tools it callsOnly if you turn MCP on

Metadata providers never receive file paths, file names, sizes or hashes. Lyrics are read from local files only. Velora never modifies your media.
