Questions and answers.

General

What is Velora?

A self-hosted media server for the Mac with apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Vision Pro and CarPlay, plus a web app. It plays your own movies, shows, music, audiobooks, books, manga, papers and sheet music.

How much does it cost?

Velora is free to download during the beta. If it's useful to you, you can sponsor it on GitHub.

Is Velora open source?

No. Velora is closed source.

Does Velora need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything runs on your Mac and your network. Metadata lookups and cloud AI providers are optional. See Privacy.

Which languages does it support?

English, German and Japanese, across every app, the web app and the server. Velora follows your device's language.

Server and media

How should I name and organize my files?

One folder per kind of media, one folder per title, the year in the name, and S01E01 numbers for episodes. The guide to organizing your media has examples for every library type, extras, backdrops, artwork and subtitles. Jellyfin and Plex layouts work as they are.

Will Velora change or move my files?

No. Velora only reads your media folders. Artwork, previews, conversions and generated audio go into Velora's own data folder.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

No. Velora Server needs Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

Can I keep using Jellyfin?

Yes. Velora Server reads Jellyfin-style folders, .nfo files and local art, and keeps its own copies. The apps can also sign in to a Jellyfin server. See Jellyfin.

Can I use Velora with my Plex or Navidrome server?

Yes. In the apps, choose Other server and type its address; Velora works out whether it's Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome. Plex signs in on plex.tv, so Velora never sees your Plex password. See Plex and Navidrome.

Can I watch away from home?

Yes, through a remote address you add to the connection, such as a VPN or a port you forward yourself. Velora doesn't run a relay.

What happened to the wall dashboard?

It was removed in 2.6. Live Traffic is still in the Mac server app.

Apps

Where are the apps?

The apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro are in TestFlight beta. CarPlay is waiting for Apple's approval.

Why Apple devices only?

So every app can be truly native. Other devices can use the web app. See Compare.

Can several people use one server?

Yes. Each account has its own progress, playlists, pins, highlights, Replay and recommendations, and admins choose which libraries each person sees.

Does everyone in the house share the skip and autoplay settings?

No. Skip intros, skip credits, autoplay next episode, play the next song and the sheet-music count-in are each person's own. They're saved to the account on Velora Server, so all of that person's devices use them.

Can I download for offline use?

Yes: movies, episodes, music, audiobooks, books, papers and manga.

AI

Does the AI send my library anywhere?

Recommendations, narration voices and music generation run on your Mac. Only if you choose a cloud provider for scripts or lyrics does that provider receive the text it needs.

What do I need for music generation?

An Apple silicon Mac with enough memory; we recommend 16 GB or more. The admin turns it on and sets limits on the server's Music Generation page.

Can I use audio made with Create Audio commercially?

Not with Phonon 40M, which is licensed CC BY-NC (non-commercial).

Who can use the AI features?

Admins decide, per user, who can create audio and music, and how many songs each person can make per day.
