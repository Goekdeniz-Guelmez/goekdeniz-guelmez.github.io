Changelog

What's new in Velora.

Every release, newest first. The server and the apps share a version number.

Velora 2.6.27 20 Nov 2026

Fixed

  • Sound and picture stay in sync when you continue a show or movie where you left off, on every device. The 2.6.23 fix didn't reach the players: on videos Velora repackages instead of converting, the sound could still run up to several seconds ahead after resuming, also with Dolby Digital audio passed through unchanged.
  • Mac: Close and Back at the top left of the player, the readers and Now Playing react to clicks again, and the full-screen button sits in the top bar instead of covering AirPlay.
  • The Create Audio sheets (podcast, audiobook and more) open at once on big libraries instead of freezing the app, and pages redraw faster everywhere.
  • Mac: the Music page shows music only, without the Music | Audiobooks switch (audiobooks are in Books).

Velora 2.6.26 19 Nov 2026

Apps

  • The Mac app is made for the Mac: Movies and Shows have their own pages (also on iPad and Vision Pro), posters show a Play button when you point at them, items have right-click menus, and keyboard shortcuts cover search (⌘F), back and forward (⌘[ and ⌘]) and moving through grids with the arrow keys and Return. The player shows its controls when you move the pointer and goes full screen with a double-click.
  • Now Playing takes far less power: its moving smoke background needs about 25 times less graphics work on iPhone and 65 times less on Apple TV, and the player, lyrics, audiobook and transcript screens no longer redraw everything several times a second. Apple TV pages draw their misty background once instead of 15 times a second.

Fixed

  • Metadata services are only asked while their switch is on. Turning Google Books off didn't stop ebook lookups, and the OMDb and Fanart.tv switches looked off while they were on and waiting for a key.

Velora 2.6.25 19 Nov 2026

New

  • Automatic is now the default language when you create audio: Velora takes the book's language from its details or its opening chapters and narrates with the Multilingual voice. You can still pick a language yourself.

Fixed

  • Phonon voices download again once you've accepted their terms on Hugging Face. The AI page says what's missing (a token, a token that was turned down, or terms not accepted), has a Get Access button, shows real progress and only downloads the files the voice needs.
  • Renaming or removing an account, a library or a folder, revoking a token or clearing a cache shows its confirmation once instead of twice.

Velora 2.6.24 19 Nov 2026

Apple TV

  • Movie and show pages: pressing Down always reaches Play, Extras, seasons and episodes, also with a moving backdrop. Show pages scroll again, and the logo and full description are back.

Fixed

  • Background work no longer waits in one line. New episodes, movie details and moving backdrops are no longer held up for hours behind thousands of book lookups; books, papers and moving backdrops each have their own queue.
  • When Google Books or another service says it's busy, Velora waits and tries again instead of giving up. Books that missed their details this way are looked up again.
  • Your place is saved even after a long pause or a server restart, in the apps and in the web player.
  • Pausing background work or quitting Velora Server no longer cancels a running job such as an audiobook being narrated; it carries on later.
  • The web app ships inside Velora Server, so it no longer disappears after a while.
  • Scrubbing previews for videos that had none, such as some disc extras.
  • In the web player, converted audio resumes at the right spot.
  • Finished background jobs older than two weeks are cleaned up.

Velora 2.6.23 19 Nov 2026

New

  • Episode order per show (Velora Server; the apps stay at 2.6.22). Some shows' discs follow another order than TMDB, which counts the original broadcast: Cowboy Bebop, Firefly, Futurama. In Velora Server, open Metadata & Artwork, choose the show under Episode order and pick the order your files use, for example Blu-ray Order. Names, descriptions and episode pictures then match your files.

Fixed

  • Audio stays in sync when you resume a video partway through. On videos Velora repackages instead of converting, the sound could be up to a second ahead.
  • Refreshing metadata keeps each episode's picture from TMDB instead of replacing it with a frame from the video.
  • With Save artwork next to media on, Velora no longer reads its own saved pictures back as yours, so it can update them later.

Velora 2.6.22 18 Nov 2026

Automations

  • New in the apps under your profile: let your server research a question every morning (a report or an audio briefing), turn every new paper or book into a podcast or summary, or make a song of the day. Each person has their own, and they run on the server while your devices are off.

AI assistants

  • Integrations has switches per account for each library and each AI tool (Deep Research, Create Audio, Music Generation, Automations). When on, assistants such as Claude can start research, create audio and songs, and set up automations.
  • Claude Code connects straight to your server at /mcp, and the Allowed tools switches are now enforced.

Improved

  • Downloaded items show an orange checkmark; the arrow only means download. Downloads keep their artwork and details, so they show with covers offline.
  • Home: pulling down to reload shows the spinner like every other page and never empties your library. On iPad, Home has the profile button again.

Velora 2.6.21 18 Nov 2026

New

  • Moving backdrops for TV shows: a show's backdrops/Backdrop.mkv plays behind the show page on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro, and with sound on Apple TV.

Velora 2.6.20 18 Nov 2026

Fixed

  • Videos that stopped about 20 minutes in (most often on iPad) now pick up again by themselves, in every Velora app and in the web app.
  • Converted videos stream in 4-second pieces instead of 1-second ones, a quarter of the requests to the server.
  • Web app: converted videos resume at the right place.

Velora 2.6.19 18 Nov 2026

Other servers

  • Sign in to a Plex Media Server: approve Velora on plex.tv or with a code at plex.tv/link, so Velora never sees your Plex password. Movies, shows, music and photos, with versions, resume and Plex's Continue Watching.
  • Sign in to Navidrome and other Subsonic servers (Gonic, Airsonic) for your music, with playlists, favorites, lyrics and play counts.
  • One way in for all of them: choose Other server and type the address. Velora works out whether it's Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome.

Fixed

  • Jellyfin 12: MKV and other converted videos play again, and books open.
  • Title page shelves span the full width on iPad and Mac.
  • Disc menu loops no longer get scrubbing thumbnails.

Velora 2.6.0 16 Nov 2026

New

  • Music generation with YuE2-3B on MLX: Simple and Custom modes, ✦ Enhance, ✦ Write or Enhance, Advanced settings, progress and a Created by You shelf. On every app, including a simpler version on Apple TV.
  • Apple Books-style paged EPUB reader with Auto, Original, Paper, Gray and Night themes, eight fonts, text size and line spacing.
  • The apps can sign in to Jellyfin servers: watch, listen and read, with direct play or Jellyfin's transcoding.

Removed
