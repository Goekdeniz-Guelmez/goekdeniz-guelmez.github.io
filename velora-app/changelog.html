Changelog

What's new in Velora.

Every release, newest first. The server and the apps share a version number.

Velora 2.6.0 Upcoming

New

  • Music generation with YuE2-3B on MLX: Simple and Custom modes, ✦ Enhance, ✦ Write or Enhance, Advanced settings, progress and a Created by You shelf. On every app, including a simpler version on Apple TV.
  • Apple Books-style paged EPUB reader with Auto, Original, Paper, Gray and Night themes, eight fonts, text size and line spacing.
  • The apps can sign in to Jellyfin servers: watch, listen and read, with direct play or Jellyfin's transcoding.
  • Audio embeddings: EmbeddingGemma 2 now uses the sound of music and audiobooks for recommendations. It can be switched off on the AI page.

Removed

  • The web app's wall-mount dashboard. Live Traffic stays in the Mac server app.

Velora 2.5.0 16 Nov 2026

New

  • Music & Audiobooks home with pins and per-account playlists, with custom synced playlist covers.
  • The Music/Audiobooks switch appears only when both libraries exist.
  • On-device recommendations from a native Swift port of EmbeddingGemma (4/6/8-bit or full precision, chosen on the AI page).
  • Narration cast: Ava, Theo, Mira and more, with fresh personas for each audio and an optional intro and outro.
  • Auto-continue and Skip Intro.
  • Audio track listed above Subtitles in the track menu.

Velora 2.4.0 15 Nov 2026

Shows

  • Auto-play next episode: countdown card with the next episode's still, Watch Credits, “Are you still watching?” after 3 episodes, and a sleep timer.
  • Skip Intro, Skip Recap and Skip Credits on every player, from MKV chapters, Jellyfin intro data or audio matching across a season. Auto-skip is optional.

Create Audio

  • A named cast with matching voices; the speakers call each other by name.
  • Personalities created fresh for every audio, overridable per voice.
  • Intro and outro only once, with an “Intro & outro” switch.
  • A timed transcript with a Text view in every audiobook player.
  • “Generated with AI on Velora” as the author, with an AI badge.
  • Finished audiobooks are saved straight into your Audiobooks folder.
  • Runs alongside streaming by default.

Apple TV

  • Posters, backdrops and stills now load.
  • The Connect button can always be reached with the remote.

Reading

  • Select and mark text with a finger, trackpad or keyboard in papers and books: highlight colours, Underline and Add Note.

Velora 2.3.0 15 Nov 2026

Create Audio works

  • Jobs run in the background with real progress (script, voice, chapters) and a readable error if something fails.
  • Voice previews play on the server's AI page.
  • A notification on your devices and on the Mac when the audio is ready.

Player

  • Opens instantly on top of the page you're on.
  • Double-tap or pinch to switch between fill and fit.
  • The audio and subtitle menu scrolls and stays open while you choose.
  • The Live Activity is removed when playback stops.
  • Now Playing: one cover glides between artwork and lyrics/queue.

Library

  • Watched/read checkmarks and where you left off (“12 min left”, “Page 74 of 188”).
  • Filter and sort in every library.
  • Every episode with its real title; seasons, specials and extras grouped correctly.
  • Audio and subtitle tracks with proper names (language, SDH, Forced, Signs & Songs, Commentary).
  • Album page: one cover that follows your scrolling.

Downloads

  • Video downloads play on iPhone and iPad: MKVs are converted to MP4, with Original or Smaller quality.
  • Easier removal, including Remove All.

Velora 2.2.0 15 Nov 2026

Playback

  • Videos start in well under a second.
  • The player opens instantly with a black loading screen, and shows Retry and Close if something fails.
  • Image subtitles (PGS/VobSub) never turn on automatically and are labelled “burned in, slower”.

Reading

  • Download books, PDFs, papers, audiobooks and manga, and read them offline.
  • Highlights, notes and Pencil drawings are saved to your account only and sync across devices, offline-first.
  • iPhone reader opens in reading mode, with Markup, Undo/Redo and Done.

Music

  • Albums list every song correctly.
  • New album page where the cover shrinks into the corner as you scroll, and a new album page on Apple TV.
  • Now Playing remembers lyrics, queue or artwork.

Fixes

  • Whole buttons are tappable.
  • Detail pages and Library tiles no longer overflow.
  • Create Audio follows the server's “Who can create” setting.
  • Book and paper covers are made locally right away.

Velora 2.1.3 15 Nov 2026 · Apps 2.1.2

Faster

  • Videos with subtitles start in about a second.
  • The player opens instantly on every device, with Retry and Close if a start fails.

Artwork

  • Show artwork from TVmaze, Kitsu and AniList without an API key.
  • A reminder to add a free TMDB key for the best movie posters.

Fixed

  • Create Audio works with Ollama :cloud models through a local Ollama.
  • Apple Pencil in the iPad reader uses Apple's markup, tool picker and text menu.
  • The server reports its real version.

New

  • Request log with a Show Logs button on the Playback page.

Velora 2.1.2 15 Nov 2026 · Apps 2.1.1

Faster

  • Movies and shows start in well under a second on the local network, and far seeks open a new stream at the target.
  • Resume no longer waits; saved positions are correct again.
  • Background jobs pause while a video streams.
  • H.264/HEVC are copied; only DTS/TrueHD audio or MPEG-2 video is converted.

Fixed

  • Album art per album from the folder or embedded art.
  • TV shows appear in the apps with a Shows section.
  • Home banner uses the backdrop with a readable fade.
  • Apple Pencil draws in the iPad reader while fingers scroll.
  • Multi-disc albums use the album name.

New

  • Full-screen liquid smoke in Now Playing, in the album's colours, moving with the music.

Velora 2.1.1 15 Nov 2026

Fixed

  • Library scans and artwork downloads no longer stall on audio files with large metadata. Probes time out after 2 minutes.
  • Multi-disc albums use the album name instead of “Disc N”.

Velora 2.1.0 15 Nov 2026

New

  • Manga: a new library type with readers on every platform.
  • Automatic light and dark mode everywhere.
  • The smoke orb in Now Playing.
  • Every audio language and subtitle can be selected while watching, MKV included.
  • Papers: a section of their own, grouped by subject.

Metadata and artwork

  • Artwork and metadata for every type, from providers you can switch on or off; only titles and IDs are sent.
  • Existing Jellyfin libraries work as they are: season folders, .nfo files and local art.
  • Title logos replace text titles wherever a logo is available.

Velora 2.0.0 15 Nov 2026

First release

  • Velora Server for Mac: link media folders across drives; native admin app with live traffic, storage, users and AI settings.
  • Web app.
  • Narrated audiobook creation with Phonon TTS.
  • MCP server for AI assistants.
  • Apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro in TestFlight.
  • English, Deutsch, 日本語.
