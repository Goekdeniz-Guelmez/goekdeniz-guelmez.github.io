For investors

The home media server for the Apple household.

Many people live inside Apple's ecosystem, and many of them own a Mac that sits idle most of the day. Velora turns that Mac into a private media server with apps on every Apple screen, without a cloud to run and without a company account.

The problem

Personal media servers are built to run everywhere, so they feel native nowhere. Apple users put up with cross-platform apps, company accounts in the middle of their own media, and features behind subscriptions.

The product

A native Mac server plus SwiftUI apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro, and a web app. Video, music, audiobooks, books, manga and papers in one library, with AI that runs on the Mac.

Why now

Apple silicon put a hardware media engine and a capable neural engine in every Mac sold since 2020. Velora uses both: VideoToolbox for transcoding, MLX for recommendations, narration and music.
