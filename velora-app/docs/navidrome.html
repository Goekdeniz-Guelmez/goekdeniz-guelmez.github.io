Navidrome

The Velora apps can sign in to a Navidrome server and play its music, next to your Velora, Jellyfin and Plex servers. The same sign-in works with other servers that speak the Subsonic API, such as Gonic and Airsonic.

Sign in

On the Connect screen, or under Add Server…, choose Other server and type the server's address. Velora works out whether it's a Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome server and shows what it found, for example “Jellyfin 12.4”. Without a port it tries each server's usual port. If it can't tell, pick the type from the menu next to the address.

Navidrome addresses default to port 4533. Sign in with your Navidrome username and password, or with an API key if your server offers OpenSubsonic API keys. The password or key is stored in the Keychain on the device; requests carry a salted token instead of the password.

What works

  • Artists, albums (with disc headings on multi-disc albums), songs, genres, Recently Added and search, with Navidrome's cover art.
  • Songs the device can play (MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV) stream as they are. Other formats, such as Opus or Ogg Vorbis, are converted to MP3 by Navidrome.
  • Plays count on the server: Navidrome shows what's playing and counts a play once you've heard half the song or four minutes. If you connected Last.fm or ListenBrainz in Navidrome, it passes them on.
  • Playlists are Navidrome's own: create, rename, add songs or whole albums, reorder, remove and delete. Other Navidrome apps see the same playlists.
  • Favorites are Navidrome's stars for songs, albums and artists.
  • Synced lyrics when Navidrome has timings (OpenSubsonic), otherwise plain lyrics.
  • Downloads for offline listening, in the original format.

Music only. When all your servers are Navidrome servers, the apps show Music and Search. Home and Library appear once you add a Velora, Jellyfin or Plex server; Books and Manga with a Velora or Jellyfin server.

Velora-only features are hidden on a Navidrome server: Create Audio, music generation, Created by You, Replay, remote control and Velora's recommendations. Pins stay on the device, playlists can't be starred, and playlist covers come from Navidrome.

Known limitations: on Apple TV you can browse and play Navidrome music, but its playlists, stars and lyrics don't show there yet.
