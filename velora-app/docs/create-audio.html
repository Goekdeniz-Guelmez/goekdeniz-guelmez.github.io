Create Audio

Create Audio turns books, PDFs, papers, slides, notes and web pages into a chaptered audiobook, a summary, a lecture or a conversation between hosts. Speech is generated on your Mac by Phonon TTS running on MLX. Find it under Audiobooks → + on any device, or Create Audio in the web app.

Admin setup

  1. Connect a language model on the AI page: Nativ or Ollama on your Mac, or a cloud provider with your own key.
  2. Download Phonon voices and preview them.
  3. Under Audiobook Creation, choose who can create, the longest length and the daily limit.

The cast

Voices have names: Ava, Mira and Lena are female; Theo, Jonas and Sam are male. In conversations the speakers call each other by name, and their personalities are created fresh for every audio. You can override a voice's personality, and switch the intro and outro on or off.

How a job runs

  • Jobs run in the background with progress for script, voice and chapters, and resume after a restart.
  • You get a notification on your devices and the Mac when it's ready.
  • The audiobook is saved into your Audiobooks folder, marked “Generated with AI on Velora”, with a timed transcript.
