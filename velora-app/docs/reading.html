Books, papers, manga and sheet music

Everything you read, named so Velora finds the right author, subject, series and composer.

Books

Books/
└── Lena Hart/                                  ← author
    ├── The Harbour Letters.epub
    └── North of the Light (2022)/
        └── North of the Light.epub
  • Put books in a folder named after the author; the file name is the title. A folder per book inside the author folder works too.
  • EPUB opens in the paged reader with themes and fonts. PDFs in a Books library open in the page reader.
  • The cover comes from inside the EPUB. A cover.jpg only belongs in a folder that holds a single book.
  • Details come from Open Library and Google Books when they're switched on: the title and author are sent, or the ISBN.

Papers

Papers/
├── Machine Learning/                           ← subject
│   ├── Attention Over Tides (2023).pdf
│   └── Sparse Harbours (2024).pdf
└── Oceanography/
    └── Wave Memory in Shallow Water.pdf
  • The folder a paper is in is its subject; the Papers page groups by it.
  • Name the file with the paper's title (and year, if you like). arXiv and Crossref then find the authors and abstract.
  • Highlights, notes and Apple Pencil drawings are saved in your account, never written into the PDF.

Manga

Manga/
└── Moonlit Courier/                            ← series
    ├── cover.jpg
    ├── Moonlit Courier v01.cbz                 ← volume 1
    ├── Moonlit Courier v02.cbz
    └── Vol 03/
        ├── Chapter 015.cbz                     ← volume 3, chapter 15
        └── Chapter 016/                        ← a folder of images
            ├── 001.jpg
            ├── 002.jpg
            └── …
  • The top folder is the series. Author/Series/ works too.
  • Volumes: v01, Vol 1, Volume 1, Band 1, Tome 1. Chapters: c010, Ch 10, Chapter 10, #10, Kapitel 10, 第10話. A file with only a number is read as a chapter.
  • Files: CBZ, CBR, CB7, ZIP or PDF, or a folder of images. Number the pages with leading zeros (001.jpg) so they stay in order.
  • Right-to-left is the default; switch to single page, two pages or webtoon scrolling in the reader.

Sheet music

Sheet Music/
├── Johann Sebastian Bach/                      ← composer
│   ├── Prelude in C Major BWV 846.mxl
│   └── Goldberg Variations/
│       └── Aria.pdf                            ← composer: Bach
├── Erik Satie/
│   ├── Gymnopédie No. 1.musicxml
│   └── Gymnopédie No. 1.mid
└── Frédéric Chopin - Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2.pdf  ← loose: “Composer - Title”
  • A folder per composer. Deeper folders (a collection, an opera) still take the composer from the top folder.
  • Inside a composer folder the whole file name is the title, dashes included (La Traviata - Introduzione). Only loose files at the top use Composer - Title.
  • MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl) is the best format: it's drawn as music and plays at once with the cursor, loops, parts and count-in. Export it from MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius or Finale.
  • MIDI opens as notation too; rhythms are approximate, since MIDI stores a performance.
  • PDF opens in the page reader. Make Interactive reads its notes so it plays too; an automation can do this for every new PDF score.
  • .musicxml and .mxl files are picked up in any library; PDF, MIDI and .xml files are scores only in a Sheet Music library.
