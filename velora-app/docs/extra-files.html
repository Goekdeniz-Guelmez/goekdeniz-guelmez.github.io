Artwork, subtitles and other files

Every file Velora reads next to your media, in one place. Velora only reads these files; it never changes or deletes them.

Artwork

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC, upper or lower case. <file> means the media file's name without its extension. The first match in each row wins, and local pictures always win over online ones.

PictureNamesUsed for
Poster<file>-poster, poster, movie, folder, cover, showMovies, shows, seasons
Backdrop<file>-fanart, <file>-backdrop, fanart, backdrop, background, art, landscapePage headers, Apple TV
Title logo<file>-logo, <file>-clearlogo, logo, clearlogoShown over the backdrop instead of the title text
Season posterseason01-poster, season1-poster, season-specials-poster; or poster in the season folderShows
Episode still<file>-thumb, <file>, <file>-landscapeEpisode rows
Cover<file>-cover, cover, folder, front, album, albumartAlbums, audiobooks, books, manga, scores

Save artwork next to media (Metadata & Artwork in Velora Server, off by default) is the one exception to “read only”: it copies the pictures Velora found online into your folders under these names, so Jellyfin, Kodi, Plex and Finder see them too. It never replaces a picture that's already there, and it marks its copies so Velora can still update them later.

Subtitles

SRT, ASS, SSA and WebVTT files named like the video, with optional parts between dots:

Ember Coast (2019).mkv
Ember Coast (2019).srt                    ← no language given
Ember Coast (2019).en.srt                 ← English
Ember Coast (2019).de.forced.srt          ← German, forced (signs and foreign dialogue)
Ember Coast (2019).English.sdh.srt        ← English, for the deaf and hard of hearing
Ember Coast (2019).ja.Commentary.ass      ← Japanese, titled “Commentary”
PartMeans
en, eng, English, de, ja, …The language (two or three-letter code, or the English name)
forced, foreignForced: only signs and foreign-language lines
sdh, cc, hiSDH: with sound descriptions
Any other wordBecomes the track's name, for example Commentary or Signs

The name must start with the video's full name followed by a dot: Ember Coast (2019) 2.srt belongs to a different file. Subtitle tracks inside an MKV are listed with their names as well.
