Music generation

Velora creates songs with YuE2-3B, ported natively to MLX. Songs are made on the server Mac and appear in the creator's library.

Turn it on (admin)

  1. Open Music Generation in Velora Server and enable it.
  2. Pick a precision: 4, 6 or 8-bit. The model is quantized on your Mac from the original weights on first use, then cached.
  3. Choose a schedule (now, low priority or only when idle), the save folder, the longest song, the songs per user per day, and which inputs are allowed.
  4. Make sure a language model is connected on the AI page; Simple mode and the ✦ buttons use it.

Make a song

On iPhone, iPad, Mac or Vision Pro, tap ✦+ on the Music home.

  • Simple: describe the song in a line. The language model fills in the title, style, lyrics and length.
  • Custom: write the style and lyrics yourself with [verse], [chorus] and [bridge] sections, or tick Instrumental. ✦ Enhance improves the style; ✦ Write or Enhance drafts or polishes the lyrics.
  • Advanced: Steps (more is cleaner but slower), Planning (melody and chords, melody only, or off) and Guidance (how closely the song follows the style).

On Apple TV you can describe a song, choose Instrumental and pick a Quality (8, 16, 32 or 64 steps).

While it runs

One song is made at a time. Progress shows in the app and on the server's Activity page. The job pauses while a video streams, and the model is unloaded shortly after it finishes. Finished songs are saved per account with a generated cover and appear under Created by You.

Music generation is the heaviest AI feature. We recommend a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more.
