Plex

The Velora apps can sign in to a Plex Media Server, next to your Velora, Jellyfin and Navidrome servers. Plex has movies, shows, music and photos; it has no books or manga.

Sign in

On the Connect screen, or under Add Server…, choose Other server and type the server's address. Velora works out whether it's a Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome server and shows what it found, for example “Jellyfin 12.4”. Without a port it tries each server's usual port. If it can't tell, pick the type from the menu next to the address.

For Plex, Velora never sees your Plex password. Choose Sign in with Plex to approve Velora on plex.tv in a browser sheet, or Use a code on another device and enter the code at plex.tv/link on any phone or computer. On Apple TV, the code comes with a QR code. Without an address, Velora lists the servers on your Plex account. You can also sign in with an address and a Plex token.

Velora tries every address Plex knows for your server and uses the best one that answers: your home network first, then a remote address, then Plex's relay. The others are kept for when you're away from home.

What works

  • Movies, shows, music and photos, with Plex's artwork. A music library named Audiobooks shows as audiobooks.
  • Movies with several files or editions get a version picker.
  • Files the device can play are direct-played. Otherwise Plex converts them, with the audio track and subtitles you chose.
  • Resume points and watched state go back to Plex, so Plex's own Continue Watching stays in step. Skip Intro and Credits use Plex's markers.
  • Continue Watching, Recently Added, collections and genres.

Velora-only features are hidden on a Plex server: Create Audio, music generation, Replay, remote control, pins, playlists and Velora's recommendations. Books and Manga are hidden when all your servers are Plex servers.

Known limitations: Plex photos are read only, and Plex's free streaming channels and Live TV don't show in the apps.
