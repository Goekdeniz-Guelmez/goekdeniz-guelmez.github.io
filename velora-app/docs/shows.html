Shows

A folder for the show, a folder for each season, and S01E01 in every episode's name. That's all Velora needs to list every episode in order with its real title.

The basic layout

Shows/
└── Harbor Lights (2021)/
    ├── Season 01/
    │   ├── Harbor Lights - S01E01 - Pilot.mkv
    │   └── Harbor Lights - S01E02 - The Keeper.mkv
    └── Season 02/
        └── Harbor Lights - S02E01.mkv
  • The show folder names the show, whatever the episode files say. Add the year when a show has a remake or a namesake.
  • The episode title after the number is optional: Velora takes real titles from TMDB or TVmaze.
  • Episodes may also sit directly in the show folder, as long as their names carry S01E01.

Season folders and specials

Season folders can be called Season 01, Season 1, S01, Staffel 1, Saison 1 or Temporada 1. Specials go in Specials or Season 00, numbered S00E01, S00E02, in the order TMDB lists them.

Extras and moving backdrops

  • Extras go in an Extras folder (or Featurettes, Trailers, Interviews, the same names as for movies) in the show folder. They show on the show's page, not as episodes.
  • A moving backdrop goes in backdrops/Backdrop.mkv, or as Backdrop.mkv directly in the show folder. Box sets often have one menu loop per disc: put them all in the backdrops folder as Backdrop.mkv, Backdrop 2.mkv, Backdrop 3.mkv.

Skip Intro and Skip Credits

Velora finds intros, recaps, credits and previews in four ways:

  1. Chapters in the file. Chapters named Opening, OP, Intro or Vorspann, and Ending, ED, Credits or Abspann, work at once.
  2. Jellyfin's segment data, if Jellyfin's Intro Skipper already found them.
  3. An .edl file next to the episode (<file>.edl, as Intro Skipper writes it).
  4. Audio matching across the season. Velora compares the episodes' sound and finds the part they share. It works best with the whole season in one folder.

Whether the intro and credits are skipped automatically is each person's choice under Settings › Playback.

Episode order

TMDB numbers some shows by their original broadcast, while the discs follow another order (Cowboy Bebop, Firefly, Futurama). If episodes get the names of other episodes, keep your files as they are: open Metadata & Artwork in Velora Server, choose the show under Episode order and pick the order your files use, for example Blu-ray Order. Names, descriptions and episode pictures then match your files.

Artwork

PictureWhere and how
Show poster, backdrop, logoIn the show folder: poster, folder or show; fanart or backdrop; logo or clearlogo
Season posterIn the show folder: season01-poster.jpg, season-specials-poster.jpg; or poster.jpg inside the season folder
Episode stillNext to the episode: <file>-thumb.jpg or <file>.jpg. Without one, Velora uses TMDB's still or a frame from the video.

Details from .nfo files

tvshow.nfo in the show folder describes the show; <file>.nfo next to an episode describes that episode. Velora reads both, and they win over online details.
