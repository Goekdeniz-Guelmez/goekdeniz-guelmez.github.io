Getting started

Velora runs on one Mac (the server) and you use it from your other devices (the apps).

1. Install Velora Server

Download VeloraServer.dmg, open it and drag Velora Server into Applications. You need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Installing ffmpeg (for example with Homebrew) lets Velora transcode formats your devices can't play directly.

2. Create your admin account

Open Velora Server. The first launch asks you to create the admin account. This only works on the Mac itself, never over the network.

3. Link your media folders

In Folders, add the folders that hold your media, on any drive. Velora recognises the usual layouts for movies, shows, music, audiobooks, books and papers. Velora never writes to your media folders: artwork, previews and generated audio go into Velora's own data folder.

4. Connect your devices

Open the Velora app on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV or Vision Pro. Your server appears automatically under On this network. Pick it, sign in once, and the device stays connected. For access away from home, add the server's remote address to the connection.
