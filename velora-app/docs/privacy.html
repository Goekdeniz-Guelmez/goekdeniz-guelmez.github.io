Privacy

Velora is self-hosted. Your media, your accounts, your history and your highlights live on your Mac. There's no Velora cloud and no telemetry.

What can leave your network, and when

FeatureSent toWhat is sentWhen
Movie and TV metadataTMDB, TVmaze, Kitsu, AniListTitle, year, season and episodeOnly for providers you leave on
Music metadata and coversMusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, TheAudioDBArtist, album, trackOnly if enabled
Book, paper and manga metadataOpen Library, Google Books, arXiv, Crossref, MangaDexTitle, author, ISBN or DOIOnly if enabled
Scripts, Simple-mode songs, lyric help, cleanupThe language model provider your admin choseThe text needed for the taskOnly with a cloud provider. Nativ and Ollama stay on your Mac.
Model downloadsHugging FaceDownload requestWhen EmbeddingGemma 2, YuE2-3B or Phonon voices are downloaded
JellyfinThe Jellyfin server you addYour Jellyfin sign-in and playback reportsOnly if you add one
Plexplex.tv and the Plex server you addThe plex.tv sign-in (a code you approve; never your password), what's playing and resume pointsOnly if you add one
NavidromeThe Navidrome or Subsonic server you addA salted sign-in token, what's playing and playsOnly if you add one
MCPThe assistant you connectResults of the tools it callsOnly if you turn MCP on

Recommendations, narration voices and music generation run on your Mac. Metadata providers never receive file paths, file names, sizes or hashes. Lyrics are read from local files only. Velora never modifies your media.
