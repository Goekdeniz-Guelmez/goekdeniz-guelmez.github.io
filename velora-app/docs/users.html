Users and devices

Everyone in your household can have their own account. Manage them in Users & Devices in Velora Server.

Accounts and roles

  • Administrator: everything, including server settings.
  • Member: watches, listens and reads the libraries you allow.

For each account you choose which libraries it sees, and whether it may create audio and music. Admins can reset a password to a temporary one that the person changes at next sign-in.

What stays separate per person

Progress and watched state, playlists and pins, highlights and notes, downloads, Replay and recommendations.

Devices

A device signs in once and stays signed in. Every device is listed under its account with when it was last active, and you can sign out any device from the server. Sign-in tokens are stored in the device's Keychain.

Limits

For music generation you can set how many songs each person can make per day. Create Audio has its own “Who can create” setting.
