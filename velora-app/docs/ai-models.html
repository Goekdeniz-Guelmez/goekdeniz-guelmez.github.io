AI models

Velora's AI features are set up on the AI page of Velora Server. Models that run on your Mac use MLX and are downloaded once into Velora's data folder. Heavy jobs run at background priority and pause while a video streams.

Language model connections

Used for audiobook scripts, Simple-mode songs, lyric help and library cleanup.

  • On your Mac: Nativ, or Ollama (including :cloud models through a local Ollama).
  • Cloud: OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Azure or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with your own key.

Pick a default model, and optionally a different one per feature.

Recommendations: EmbeddingGemma 2

A native Swift port of EmbeddingGemma 2 on MLX turns titles, descriptions, covers and audio into vectors. Choose 4, 6 or 8-bit or full precision. Until the download finishes, Apple's on-device model is used. The setting Use audio for music and audiobooks adds the audio model; turn it off to save memory.

Music generation: YuE2-3B

See Music generation.

Voices: Phonon TTS

Download narration voices on the AI page and preview them. See Create Audio.

Only one heavy model runs at a time: music generation and recommendations never run together.
