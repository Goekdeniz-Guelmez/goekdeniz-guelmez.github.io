Multi-server

One Velora app can be signed in to several Velora servers, for example yours and a family member's, and show one merged library.

  • The same title on two servers appears once. Velora matches by provider IDs, or by title, year and length when unsure, and doesn't merge when in doubt.
  • When you press play, Velora picks the best source: reachable, then direct play, then quality, then local network before remote. If a server drops out, it switches to the next one.
  • Your progress is saved to the server you played from and mirrored to the others.
  • Use the server picker next to your profile to see All Servers or just one.
