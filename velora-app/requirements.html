System requirements

What you need.

One Apple silicon Mac for the server, and the Apple devices you already have.

Server

ItemRequirement
MacApple silicon (M1 or later). Intel Macs are not supported.
macOSmacOS 26 or later
StorageYour media on that Mac or on drives connected to it. Velora's data folder holds artwork, previews and caches; the Storage page shows how much.
NetworkYour devices on the same network. Remote access works through an address you add yourself.
Optional: ffmpegFor converting formats a device can't play directly.
Optional: TMDB keyA free key gives the best movie posters. Shows, music, books and manga work without one.

AI features

All AI features are optional and off until you set them up. Models are downloaded once into Velora's data folder.

FeatureModelNotes
RecommendationsEmbeddingGemma 2 (4, 6, 8-bit or full precision)Loaded only while it refreshes. Apple's on-device model is used until the download finishes.
Music generationYuE2-3B (4, 6 or 8-bit)The heaviest feature. We recommend a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more.
Create Audio voicesPhonon TTS on MLXPhonon 40M is CC BY-NC: audio made with it is for non-commercial use.
Scripts and lyricsA language model you chooseNativ or Ollama on your Mac, or a cloud provider with your own key.
