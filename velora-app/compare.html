Velora, Plex and Jellyfin.

Plex and Jellyfin are excellent media servers that run almost anywhere. Velora makes a different trade: it runs only on the Mac and only for Apple devices, so it can use what Apple builds into every one of them. Here is an honest side-by-side.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Velora if your home runs on Apple devices, you have an Apple silicon Mac, and you want one library for video, music, audiobooks, books, manga and papers, with AI that stays on your Mac.
  • Choose Plex if you need apps on almost every device ever made, or want its free streaming channels.
  • Choose Jellyfin if you want a free, open-source server on Linux or a NAS, or a big plugin ecosystem.
  • Use both: Velora's apps can sign in to a Jellyfin server, and Velora Server can read the same folders. See Jellyfin.

Why only Apple?

So every app can be truly native: Apple's player, Liquid Glass, Live Activities, Top Shelf, SharePlay and the share sheet. The server uses VideoToolbox, the hardware media engine in every Apple silicon Mac, and runs its AI with MLX. A Mac mini on a shelf is quiet and uses little power when idle, so you don't need a separate NAS or a PC running all day.
